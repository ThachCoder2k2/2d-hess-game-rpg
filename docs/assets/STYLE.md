# STYLE — phong cách và quy tắc giao file

File này được tự sinh từ `style.json`. Mọi prompt ảnh bắt đầu bằng đúng khối phong cách dưới đây; mọi prompt âm thanh bắt đầu bằng đúng bản tóm tắt âm thanh.

## Khối phong cách (style block) — chép vào đầu mọi prompt ảnh

```text
THE UNBOUND PAWN - shared art style. It applies to every image file; the group and file text below only adds what is specific to that one file.

WORLD AND MOOD. A child's bedroom turned into a serious chess war, seen at pawn scale in warm dusk lamplight. Only toy and bedroom materials exist: lathe-turned painted wood and matte plastic chess pieces, felt, woven rug wool, paper, cardboard, crayon wax, chalk, graphite, rubber eraser, tin and brass. The tone is tender and quiet: a serious war on a warm playground, humour only from scale and material, never gore, never grotesque, never glossy 3D or photographic. The look to match is the owner-approved chess family (smooth symmetric lathe-turned Staunton silhouettes, flat three-band shading, clean outline) and the warm toy-track tiles.

RESOLUTION AND PIXEL SCALE. The game renders at 640x360 with nearest-neighbour scaling, and EVERY image in the game is drawn at ONE pixel scale: 1x1. One art pixel is exactly one delivered pixel, for everything: chess pieces, bosses, tiles, props, pickups, weapons, world effects, vista parts, UI, HUD, icons, portraits and the child's hand. Nothing is drawn on a smaller grid and enlarged, and no pixel is wider or taller than one canvas pixel unless the picture itself puts a flat area there. Every file is delivered at exactly the canvas size stated for it: a PNG with alpha, hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no sub-pixel rotation, no resampling. Because every canvas pixel is a real pixel, the detail budget is the canvas itself: a 32x32 chess piece or tile has 32x32 individually placed pixels (1,024 of them) to work with, so single-pixel work is expected: one-pixel eye glints, rivets, stitches, grain lines, woven threads, seam lines, chipped corners, a one-pixel outline. A flat area is flat on purpose, never because the grid is coarse. Every measure written in a description (sizes, radii, offsets, squash, stroke widths) is in canvas pixels, which are art pixels; odd and even measures are both valid, and a '1 px' outline, hairline or stroke is exactly one pixel wide.

VIEW (one convention for the whole game). The camera looks down at about 65 degrees. Floors, rugs, tiles and flat decals are drawn from straight above. Anything that stands up shows its top surface plus a short SOUTH front face (the side toward the viewer, about a quarter of its height). Actors and standing props are drawn as FRONT ELEVATIONS facing the camera (south): lathe-turned pieces are left/right symmetric, collars and rims read as shallow bands that bulge slightly downward (the near half of an ellipse), the head ball may carry a faint top highlight, and the base rim sits on the floor at the anchor point. Never a pure side elevation, never a true top-down head, no perspective distortion beyond that. Knight heads face screen-left (west) in a three-quarter turn toward the camera on the south view; pieces have no arms, no legs and no mouths (feeling is carried by eyes, tilt and posture). Walls, cover blocks and gaps show their short front face; all other top-view tiles show none. A standing PROP keeps its base inside its 32x32 cell footprint and may rise upward out of the cell. A CHESS PIECE never does: every chess piece (the hero pawn, every enemy, every rescued or fallen white piece, and the bosses that stand on one cell) is smaller than its cell, drawn on the 32x32 canvas of ONE cell with its visible body, outline included, at least 2 px away from every canvas edge, never exactly cell-sized and never taller than the cell; relative heights follow a real chess set (pawn lowest, then knight, bishop and rook, then queen, king tallest). A boss's size is its own (owner decision 2026-10-13): the Black Pawn Captain, Knight Captain, Bishop Oracle, Queen of Tricks, Black King and the Rook Warden in its third phase stand on ONE cell like any piece; the Rook Warden in its first two phases fills a footprint of 2x2 cells (a 64x64 canvas) and the Rule Engine machine behind the Black King fills 3x3 cells (a 96x96 canvas). A body on a larger footprint obeys the same rule as a piece on one cell: it is smaller than its footprint, drawn on the canvas of the whole footprint with its visible body, outline included, at least 2 px away from every canvas edge, with its base line near the bottom of the footprint. A boss on one cell reads as a boss through its crown, silhouette details, colour and aura effects, not through size; auras and burst effects are effects, not bodies, and may extend beyond the footprint.

LIGHT. One fixed light from the UPPER LEFT (the bedroom window is in the north wall, a little west): highlights on top and left planes, mid tone on the front, shadow on the right and lower planes; contact shadows fall south-east. Actor sprites carry no cast shadow on the floor (the engine draws actor shadows separately); props carry only a tight 1-2 px contact shadow inside their own footprint.

OUTLINE AND SHADING. Actors and chess pieces: a 1 px near-black outline #14141C added OUTSIDE the body (it never shrinks the silhouette) and no inner outlines except seams the description names. Props, pickups and effects: a 1 px outline in a darker shade of the object's own material (never pure black). Tiles: no outline at all. UI: a 1 px charcoal #2B2D3A outline. Shading is exactly three flat tone bands per material (light, mid, shadow) plus at most one small highlight cluster; no gradients, no dithering (unless a description asks for a dithered edge, or it is the opacity checkerboard defined under PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY), no noise filters, no glow or bloom. Texture is hand-placed pixel clusters and deliberate marks (scuffs, stitches, grain, repairs), never random noise.

PALETTE. At most 16 colours per object (hero 14, tiles 10). These gameplay colours are reserved and each keeps ONE meaning: coral red #D84A3A = imminent damage only (the Black pieces' dull seams #9C2F2A brighten to it on the commit frame; faded toy-paint red #B8473D is a different, duller colour and is allowed on toys); dark orange #E0762A = anticipation; crayon blue #3A6FE0 = the child and what the child made (glow variant #4DA3FF for lit effects); it is never a decorative accent on neutral HUD frames, toasts, prompts, badges or world signage, which exist before the child is revealed (menu focus rings are the one documented exception); golden yellow #F2C23C = interactable and reward (glow variant #FFD34E); mint #63D68B = safe and healing; grey pencil #9A9AA3 = enemy rule amendments, and it appears ONLY as the ink of a rule an enemy rewrites or revokes (an amendment, the scribble over disabled help, a stripped direction); every other grey is a neutral material grey that a group names by its material (neutral graphite #6B7380, a district's own graphite, dust grey) and never calls 'grey pencil'. The White side is warm ivory (light #F1E8D2, mid #D9CCAE, shadow #A9B3C4 pale blue, faded cloth tie #7C8CA6); the Black side is charcoal (light #4A5060, mid #2E323C, shadow #1B1E27, cool blue-black shadows). Never pure white or pure black except on a single impact frame. Each district has a local palette of about 24 colours given in its group palette note; floor-type tiles stay mid-value and low-saturation so telegraph colours remain readable on top of them.

ANIMATION AND CONSISTENCY. Every frame of a clip uses the same canvas, the same anchor and the same palette. The anchor is the base point of the art (feet or base rim) that the engine places on the floor of the cell: for feet-anchored art it is (width/2, height-4), or (width/2, height-2) when the canvas is 32 px high or less; a chess piece's 32x32 canvas IS its cell, so its anchor (16,30), the exact base-bottom centre, lies 14 px below the cell centre (16,16) in the lower part of its own cell, and a body box is centred 0 to 3 px below the cell centre (the pawn lowest, the king exactly on it); a body on a larger boss footprint stands on the base-bottom centre of its whole canvas instead, 2 px above the bottom edge: (32,62) on a 2x2-cell footprint, (48,94) on a 3x3-cell footprint. The base never slides sideways by more than 3 px on one cell, 7 px on a 2x2-cell footprint and 11 px on a 3x3-cell footprint (the cell-to-cell slide is done in code). Anticipation is longer in feel than impact, the impact frame is the single brightest frame, squash and stretch move whole pixels only, and an effect never hides the grid cell it marks. Directional effects are drawn facing EAST (the engine rotates or flips them); hero clips are drawn for south, north and east (west is a tool mirror); enemies and bosses animate in front view only and carry facing in static back and side stills. Clip frames are saved as <folder>/anim/<clip>/NN.png, NN from 01.

PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY. A percentage written next to an element, colour or effect in any description ('glow at 60 percent', 'ring 88 px at 70%', 'fan 70% alpha', 'outline strength 85%') is that element's OPACITY: 100 percent is fully solid, 0 percent is absent, and it is never a size, a scale or a progress. A percentage means something else only when the text names what it measures ('40 percent of its height', 'the bar filled to 40 percent', 'the hold at 33 percent progress', 'foreshortened to 50 percent width'). Opacity is drawn in exactly one of two ways. (1) In a sprite with a transparent background (effects, props, UI) as a FLAT translucent fill: the element is drawn in its one colour with that single alpha value (alpha = percentage x 255, rounded) on every one of its pixels, with hard edges and no feathering; this is the only place a pixel may carry an alpha other than 0 or 255, and one element has exactly one alpha. (2) In a fully opaque tile, or wherever a description says dithered, as a one-art-pixel CHECKERBOARD of the colour over what lies beneath, or as the colour blended once into the pixels beneath it; never a gradient. When a frame lists several percentages, each belongs to the element it follows.

TEXT. No letters, numerals or words inside any image: every label is written by the game's pixel font. The only exceptions are stated in a group description (the logo, keycap legends).
```

## Điều không được vẽ (global avoid)

```text
any text, letters, numerals or watermark (except where a group description names an exception); anti-aliased or feathered edges to transparency and semi-transparent edge pixels (alpha is 0 or 255, except the flat translucent fills defined by PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY in the style above or an alpha value a group states for a translucent fill); baked drop shadows, outer glow or bloom (unless the group asks for it); more than one object per file, a sprite sheet, atlas, strip, contact sheet or collage; a background, floor, ground plane, frame, border or canvas outline behind the object; content from neighbouring cells; grid lines or visible tile borders; gradients, dithering (except the opacity checkerboard defined by PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY), noise filters, vector-smooth shapes, 3D-render or photographic look; pure white or pure black fills; mouths on chess pieces; blood, gore, real weapons, branded or copyrighted characters; crayon blue anywhere except where the child made it; coral red anywhere except imminent damage; cropping an object at the canvas edge (except seamless tiles and tileable strips); sub-pixel offsets, mixed pixel sizes inside one file, or art drawn at another scale and resized
```

### Điều không được vẽ, biến thể `surface`

Biến thể `surface` (mọi tile, nền bản đồ, ô giấy lặp, mảnh vista đặc): vật được vẽ CHÍNH LÀ mặt sàn/tường nên cho phép vẽ kín canvas tới cạnh, không còn cấm 'nền, sàn, khung, viền' của mặt đó.

```text
any text, letters, numerals or watermark (except where a group description names an exception); anti-aliased or feathered edges, blur, and any transparent or semi-transparent pixel (this file is fully opaque: alpha 255 on every pixel; an opacity effect inside the surface is drawn only as the one-art-pixel checkerboard or a colour blended once, as defined by PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY in the style above); baked drop shadows, outer glow or bloom (unless the group asks for it); more than one tile or surface per file, a sprite sheet, atlas, strip, contact sheet or collage, and any separate prop, character, piece or cast shadow standing on the surface (those are separate files); a second background layer, scene, room view or vignette behind or around the surface, and a frame, border or canvas outline drawn around the canvas edge (the surface itself IS the floor, wall or ground and must run edge to edge; a frame, rim, lip, cap or edge is drawn only when this file's description says the tile is one); content from neighbouring cells (draw only this cell); grid lines or visible tile borders laid over the surface (plank seams, mortar, stitching, weave and other joints that belong to the material are fine); gradients, dithering (except the opacity checkerboard defined by PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY), noise filters, vector-smooth shapes, 3D-render or photographic look; pure white or pure black fills; mouths on chess pieces; blood, gore, real weapons, branded or copyrighted characters; crayon blue anywhere except where the child made it; coral red anywhere except imminent damage; cropping that breaks a seam (the art runs to every canvas edge and continues seamlessly into a neighbouring tile wherever the TILE DATA line says so); sub-pixel offsets, mixed pixel sizes inside one file, or art drawn at another scale and resized.
```

### Điều không được vẽ, biến thể `frame`

Biến thể `frame` (mọi UI/9-patch/khung/bảng/phím, mảnh khung bản đồ, hàng rào và khung ảnh): vật được vẽ CHÍNH LÀ khung/viền/bảng nên không bị cấm vẽ khung/viền của nó, 9-patch được phép chạy tới cạnh canvas.

```text
any text, letters, numerals or watermark (except where a group description names an exception); anti-aliased or feathered edges to transparency and semi-transparent edge pixels (alpha is 0 or 255, except the flat translucent fills defined by PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY in the style above or an alpha value a group states for a translucent fill); baked drop shadows, outer glow or bloom (unless the group asks for it); more than one object per file, a sprite sheet, atlas, strip, contact sheet or collage; a scene background, floor, ground plane or room view behind the element, and any frame, border or canvas outline that this file's description does not ask for (the panel, frame, border, key cap, rail or fence that this file describes is itself the subject and must be drawn); content from neighbouring cells; 9-patch guide lines, slice markers, grid lines or visible tile borders; gradients, dithering (except the opacity checkerboard defined by PERCENTAGES AND TRANSLUCENCY), noise filters, vector-smooth shapes, 3D-render or photographic look; pure white or pure black fills; mouths on chess pieces; blood, gore, real weapons, branded or copyrighted characters; crayon blue anywhere except where the child made it; coral red anywhere except imminent damage; cropping an object at the canvas edge (except 9-patch panels, frames, border pieces and repeating edge strips whose art runs to the canvas edge as the description says); sub-pixel offsets, mixed pixel sizes inside one file, or art drawn at another scale and resized.
```

## Bản tóm tắt âm thanh (sound brief) — chép vào đầu mọi prompt âm thanh

```text
THE UNBOUND PAWN - shared audio identity. It applies to every sound file; the group and file text only adds what is specific to that file.

IDENTITY. The soundscape is the physical bedroom: small chess pieces, wood floor, paper folds, crayon and chalk scratches, toy blocks, ceramic and porcelain, rubber eraser, wooden ruler, tin, music box and soft bedroom air, plus the dangerous 'black-piece magic' of the rules (glass-paper cracks, dry ticks, hollow wooden rumbles). Never generic fantasy combat. The pawn's world is tiny, dry, close and quick; the child's hand and objects are larger, slower, heavier and softer (crayon, paper, chalk, felt). Defeated pieces make ordinary toy sounds, never gore.

FAMILIES AND TELEGRAPHS. Every telegraph family keeps its own vocabulary so the player hears the shape before seeing it: pawn = scrape and tick; knight = rising hop and a landing 'tok'; bishop = paper shimmer and a diagonal hum; rook = ruler slide and a wooden rumble; queen = restrained porcelain layers; king = glass-paper rule crack. Telegraph warnings are gameplay-critical and win the mix: player damage > enemy telegraph > player attack impact > child help > pickup and reward > ambient SFX > music. Keep the 1-4 kHz band clear in music and ambience for warnings.

MUSIC AND VOICE. Palette: toy piano, music box, celesta, glockenspiel, felt piano, pizzicato and soft bowed strings, harp, marimba, wood blocks, brushed hand percussion, low warm pads; no epic orchestra. The child's leitmotif is a music-box phrase C5 D5 E5 G5 A5 C6 (C major pentatonic); its subtraction in Act 6 is the story. Room T1 is room tone only. Act 6 and the King fight use subtraction (a bare, hollow track and hollow ambience). The ending and credits form one continuous piece in C major at 72 BPM. There is no voice acting: dialogue is short per-speaker blips on the Voice bus, never louder than SFX.

FORMAT. SFX and voice blips: WAV 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono (stereo only where the group says so), peak -1 dBFS, no clipping, no clicks, a short natural tail; variations are named <event_id>_01.wav, _02.wav ... and must sound like the same object; a looping SFX carries an embedded smpl loop chunk with a seamless join. Music and ambience: OGG Vorbis 44.1 kHz stereo, quality about 6 (roughly 192 kbps variable bitrate), music about -20 LUFS, ambience beds about -34 LUFS; a looping music or ambience file has continuous first and last samples (no click, no fade) and carries no loop tags, because the engine switches looping on when it imports the file. ONE TRACK PER AREA: the title, Act 1, the hub states, each of the five districts, each boss fight, the ending and the credits are each ONE complete mix of all their instruments, never split into stems or layers; a boss phase change is marked by a separate short WAV 'phase sting' sound effect laid over the same track.
```

## Quy tắc giao file

- MỖI FILE LÀ MỘT VẬT: một tile, một vật cảnh, một biểu tượng, một phần giao diện, một cảm xúc chân dung, một KHUNG HÌNH hoạt ảnh, một âm thanh. Không gộp bảng (sheet), atlas, dải, ảnh nhiều vật hoặc tranh cả căn phòng.
- PNG RGBA 8-bit, nền trong suốt (trừ tile: đặc hoàn toàn, vẽ kín 32x32). Đúng kích thước canvas ghi trong yêu cầu, không đổi tỉ lệ, không làm mờ, không răng cưa.
- Phần trăm trong mô tả (ví dụ 'quầng sáng 60%', 'vòng 88 px ở 70%') là ĐỘ ĐỤC của phần tử đó (100% = đặc hoàn toàn, 0% = không có), không phải kích thước hay tiến độ, trừ khi câu chữ nói rõ nó đo cái gì ('40% chiều cao', 'thanh đầy 40%'). Vẽ bằng MỘT màu phẳng với MỘT giá trị alpha (alpha = % x 255), viền cứng, không nhoè; riêng tile đặc (alpha 255) thì dùng lưới caro 1 pixel hoặc trộn màu sẵn vào pixel bên dưới. Không dùng gradient.
- Mọi ảnh vẽ pixel 1x1 (quyết định của anh, 2026-10-13): quân cờ, boss, tile, đạo cụ, hiệu ứng thế giới, giao diện, biểu tượng, chân dung và bàn tay của bé đều là một pixel vẽ = một pixel game ở độ phân giải gốc 640x360. Không vẽ trên lưới nhỏ rồi phóng to, không có khối pixel lớn hơn. Canvas 32x32 nghĩa là có đủ 32x32 pixel thật để vẽ chi tiết (đinh tán, mũi khâu, thớ gỗ, điểm sáng một pixel).
- Đặt đúng tên và đúng thư mục ghi trong yêu cầu (ghi đè lên file placeholder cùng đường dẫn thì game tự dùng, không cần sửa code).
- Hoạt ảnh: mỗi khung là một file <thư mục>/anim/<clip>/NN.png, NN bắt đầu từ 01. Mọi khung của một clip cùng canvas, cùng điểm neo, cùng bảng màu. Khung 01 cũng là ảnh tĩnh cho chế độ giảm chuyển động.
- Hướng: nhân vật chính vẽ 3 hướng (s = xuống, n = lên, e = phải), hướng trái do công cụ lật từ e. Kẻ địch và boss chỉ vẽ nhìn thẳng, kèm ảnh tĩnh body_n (quay lưng) và body_e (nhìn phải). Hiệu ứng có hướng vẽ quay sang PHẢI.
- Điểm neo (anchor): chân/đế vật phải chạm đúng điểm neo ghi trong yêu cầu, thường là (rộng/2, cao-4). Không để đế trượt ngang quá 3 px giữa các khung.
- Quân cờ nhỏ hơn ô (quyết định của anh, 2026-10-12): mọi quân cờ (pawn của anh, mọi kẻ địch và biến thể, quân trắng được cứu, Vua trắng đã ngã) vẽ trên canvas 32x32 = đúng một ô; thân quân, cả viền, cách mọi mép canvas ít nhất 2 px; không bằng ô, không cao hơn ô, không tràn sang hàng trên. Thân quân (px game): Tốt 18x22, Mã/Tượng/Xe 20x24, Hậu 22x26, Vua 22x28. Điểm neo chung: đáy đế ở (16,30). Mọi khung hoạt ảnh (nghiêng, nhún, vươn, nhảy) cũng phải nằm gọn trong lề này; vật gắn theo quân (chấm máu, mũi tên hướng, vũ khí cầm tay, bóng) cũng vừa trong một ô. Cỡ boss tuỳ boss (quyết định của anh, 2026-10-13): Đội Trưởng Tốt Đen (thân tối đa 24x22), Mã Đội Trưởng (24x24), Giáo chủ Tượng (22x24), Nữ hoàng Mánh Khóe (24x26) và Vua Đen (24x28) đứng trên MỘT ô, canvas 32x32, đế ở (16,30). Xe Cai Ngục chiếm 2x2 ô ở giai đoạn 1 và 2: canvas 64x64, thân tối đa 56x52, đế ở (32,62) sát đáy, cách mọi mép ít nhất 2 px; sang giai đoạn 3 (sau khi tự phá ngai) còn MỘT ô, canvas 32x32, thân 24x24. Cỗ máy Luật sau lưng Vua chiếm 3x3 ô: canvas 96x96, thân tối đa 92x92 (dáng thấp khoảng 88x80), đế ở (48,94), cách mọi mép ít nhất 2 px. Hào quang và hiệu ứng nổ của boss là hiệu ứng nên được tràn ra ngoài chỗ đứng.
- Màu có nghĩa riêng, không dùng sai: đỏ san hô = sát thương sắp xảy ra; cam đậm = báo trước; xanh sáp = của đứa trẻ; vàng = vật tương tác; xanh bạc hà = an toàn; bút chì xám = luật mới của kẻ địch.
