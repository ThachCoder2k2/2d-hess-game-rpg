# AU.1 Spec - Audio Identity

**Status:** draft for owner review  
**Linked lane:** Audio  
**Checklist gate:** `AU.1`

## Purpose

Audio should make the bedroom chess war feel physical and readable. It should
support timing, impact, and emotional tone.

## Audio Identity

```text
small chess pieces
wood floor
paper folds
crayon scratches
toy blocks
soft bedroom air
dangerous black-piece magic
```

## First-Chain Sound Needs

| Moment | Sound Direction |
|---|---|
| Pawn wake | soft ceramic/wood scrape, small breath of motion |
| Step | tiny base tap, not heavy footsteps |
| Sword pickup | toy/metal chime, hopeful |
| Sword swing | quick wooden/metal whoosh |
| Pawn telegraph | short warning tick or scrape |
| Hit | sharp chip/crack with tiny impact |
| Room quiet | low bedroom ambience |

## Mix Rules

- Telegraph warnings beat music in priority.
- Player hit sounds must be unmistakable.
- Pickup sounds should be pleasant but brief.
- Music should not hide warning sounds.
- Child intervention sounds should use crayon/paper/block textures.

## Completion Requirements

`AU.1` can be approved only if:

- the sound palette feels like toys/chess/bedroom materials,
- warnings are treated as gameplay-critical,
- music direction supports mood without overwhelming combat,
- and first-chain sound needs are clear.

## Cannot Approve If

- sounds feel like generic fantasy combat,
- warning sounds are optional decoration,
- music masks telegraphs,
- or child audio lacks physical material identity.
