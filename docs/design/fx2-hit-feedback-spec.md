# FX.2 Spec - Hit Feedback

**Status:** draft for owner review  
**Linked lane:** VFX And Combat Feedback  
**Checklist gate:** `FX.2`

## Purpose

Hits must feel satisfying while preserving the next combat read.

## Required Hit Feedback

- brief impact flash,
- small chip/fragment particles,
- tiny camera shake for successful hit,
- stronger feedback for player damage,
- short hit stop only when it improves clarity.

## Completion Requirements

`FX.2` can be approved only if hits confirm contact, do not hide telegraphs, and
support both player and enemy damage readability.

## Cannot Approve If

- particles cover danger cells,
- every hit shakes equally,
- or hit feedback makes timing harder to read.
