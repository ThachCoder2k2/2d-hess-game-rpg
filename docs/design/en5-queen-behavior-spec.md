# EN.5 Spec - Queen Behavior

**Status:** draft for owner review  
**Linked lane:** Enemy And Boss Design  
**Checklist gate:** `EN.5`

## Purpose

The Queen tests mastery by combining previous threat languages without turning
the board into unreadable chaos.

## Queen Combat Question

```text
Which known rule is the real threat right now?
```

## Required Behavior

| Behavior | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Threat mix | Straight, diagonal, and summoned pressure |
| Telegraph | Fewer simultaneous lanes than chess queen implies |
| Counterplay | Prioritize one threat, use learned skills |
| Story role | Controls stage and disrupts child help |

## Boss Variant: Queen Of Tricks

- Phase 1: limited straight/diagonal mix.
- Phase 2: summons/commands armed pawns.
- Phase 3: temporarily disrupts one child intervention.
- Final opening: player wins through movement and timing.

## Completion Requirements

`EN.5` can be approved only if:

- Queen combines known mechanics instead of inventing too many new ones,
- child-help disruption is dramatic but fair,
- and the fight remains readable.

## Cannot Approve If

- Queen covers too many cells at once,
- the player must memorize invisible rules,
- or disruption removes all counterplay.
