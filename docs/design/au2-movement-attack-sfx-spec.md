# AU.2 Spec - Movement And Attack SFX

**Status:** draft for owner review  
**Linked lane:** Audio  
**Checklist gate:** `AU.2`

## Purpose

Player actions should feel responsive through small physical sounds.

## Required Sounds

- pawn step tap,
- turn/shift tick if needed,
- sword swing,
- sword hit,
- Pencil thrust,
- guard/parry,
- player hurt.

## Completion Requirements

`AU.2` can be approved only if movement and attack sounds reinforce timing
without becoming noisy during repeated actions.

## Cannot Approve If

- repeated movement sounds annoy quickly,
- attack sounds do not match impact timing,
- or sounds feel like generic fantasy instead of toy/chess materials.
