# LD.5 Spec - Chess Rug Hub

**Status:** draft for owner review  
**Linked lane:** Level And World Design  
**Checklist gate:** `LD.5`

## Purpose

The Chess Rug Hub is the center of the playable bedroom world. It orients the
player, shows future routes, and gives the pawn a home base after the opening.

## Hub Requirements

- Central chess rug identity.
- Visible exits to Toybox, Bookshelf, Block Tower, Dollhouse, Under-Bed, and Desk.
- Most exits locked or blocked at first.
- Safe enough for orientation.
- Child/desk foreshadow visible.
- Fallen white pieces remind player of stakes.

## Hub Layout Sketch

```text
                 Bookshelf locked
                       ^
Toybox first <--- Chess Rug Hub ---> Block Tower locked
                       |
                Dollhouse / Bed sealed

Desk/child marks are visible near one edge, not fully accessible yet.
```

## Completion Requirements

`LD.5` can be approved only if:

- hub routes are readable,
- locked routes communicate future possibility,
- the hub feels safe enough to breathe,
- and the owner accepts the chess rug as the game's central orientation point.

## Cannot Approve If

- hub looks like a combat room only,
- future routes are hidden or confusing,
- locked doors feel arbitrary,
- or the hub does not connect visually to the bedroom.
